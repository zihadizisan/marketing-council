# Tallyhand — Q3 Growth Plan

> **Fictional.** Tallyhand is an invented company written for this example. Any resemblance
> to a real product is coincidental. The plan is deliberately average — the kind of competent,
> unremarkable document a small team actually produces — so the council has something real to
> disagree about.

## Background

Tallyhand is an invoicing and expense-tracking tool for freelance designers. We launched
quietly in February and have 180 paying users, almost all from founder word-of-mouth and one
Product Hunt post. Retention past month three is around 60%.

## Objective

Reach **1,000 paying users by 30 September**.

## Target audience

Freelancers and small business owners, aged 25–45, who are comfortable with software and
currently manage invoices in spreadsheets.

## Positioning

Tallyhand is the trusted invoicing partner for modern freelancers. We are simpler than the
enterprise accounting suites and more professional than a spreadsheet.

## Offer

$12/month, billed monthly. 14-day free trial, no card required. Annual plan at $110/year.

## Channels and budget

Total Q3 budget: **$40,000**.

| Channel | Spend | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Paid social (Instagram, LinkedIn) | $14,000 | Carousel ads showing the invoice builder |
| Google Search ads | $8,000 | Bidding on "freelance invoice software" and similar |
| Podcast sponsorship | $10,000 | One design podcast, three episodes in August |
| Content and SEO | $8,000 | Two blog posts a week, freelancing and pricing topics |

## Timeline

- **July** — paid social and search go live; blog cadence begins
- **August** — podcast sponsorship runs; first ad creative refresh
- **September** — scale whatever is working, push an annual-plan promotion

## Success metrics

We will track engagement across our social channels, follower growth, website traffic, and
blog rankings. Monthly reporting to the founders.

## Team

Two people. One marketer full-time, one designer at roughly 50% for creative production.
